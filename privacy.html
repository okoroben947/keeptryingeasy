Privacy Policy

At EasyConnect Group, your privacy and the security of your personal data are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our website, digital services, and AI automation platforms.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with seamless digital and AI-driven services, we must collect certain necessary information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Official Documentation Data: For services involving government integrations (such as NIN verification, LGA Origin Certificates, and CAC Registration), we may collect specialized identifiers like Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), National Identity Numbers (NIN), and relevant corporate documents as mandated by law.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Payment details, billing addresses, and transaction histories related to Virtual Top-Ups (VTU), electricity payments, and cable subscriptions.
  • Technical & Automated Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and interaction logs with our AI Chatbots and automated workflows.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly limit the use of your data to the following operational necessities:

  • Service Delivery: To instantly process VTU transactions, facilitate CAC registrations, retrieve WAEC/NECO certificates, and generate affidavits.
  • AI Training & Optimization: To refine our AI agents and workflows so they can better assist you. (Note: Personal identifiable information is stripped and anonymized before being used to optimize general AI behavior).
  • Customer Support: To resolve disputes, troubleshoot technical issues, and respond to your inquiries.
  • Security & Compliance: To prevent fraud, enforce our Terms and Conditions, and comply with regulatory requirements under Nigerian law.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

EasyConnect Group does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with trusted third parties necessary to fulfill your requests:

  • Government & Regulatory Bodies: Including the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) strictly for processing your official requests.
  • Service Providers: Telecommunication networks, electricity distribution companies, and payment gateways required to finalize your digital transactions.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your data if required by a subpoena, court order, or formal request from law enforcement to protect our rights or the safety of others.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption, secure API integrations, and robust firewall protocols, to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, while we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you retain the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your personal data, provided it is not required for ongoing legal or regulatory compliance (such as finalized CAC documents).
